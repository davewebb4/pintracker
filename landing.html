Features

Everything a bowler needs

🎳

Live Scoring

Tap the pins after each ball and your scorecard updates instantly. Strikes, spares, splits — all tracked automatically.

🏆

Leagues

Create leagues, bowl series, and track your season stats. Archive leagues at the end of the season without losing history.

📊

Detailed Stats

Strike rate, spare percentage, single pin and multi-pin conversions, splits — drill down into every aspect of your game.

🎯

Tournaments

Track tournament sessions separately with their own stats and history. Great for competitive events.

📱

No App Store Needed

Visit the website, add it to your home screen, and it works just like a native app — offline included.

☁️

Synced Everywhere

Your data is backed up to the cloud. Sign in on any device and your full history is right there.
